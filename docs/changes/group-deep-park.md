# Status: A group's deep park completes on every member's own evidence — 2026-10-15 (branch `fix/group-deep-park`)

Found live at MN4 (vLLM 0.30.0 and SGLang 0.5.21, TP2 over two GB10 hosts): the head's collective succeeded (`POST /sleep?level=2`, `POST /release_memory_occupation`, both 200), yet the group stayed `parking` for the whole 15-minute park step, then `park_uncertain`, `residency_handed_to_stop`, the park failing `resolved_by_owned_cleanup`. Owner decision 2026-10-15, recorded as ADR 0028's amendment of that date. Three causes:

- A group worker's Launch reported its process tree one second after the spawn, before the engine forked its rank process (vLLM's `VLLM::Worker_TP1`, SGLang's rank scheduler), so the member's recorded processes were the leader alone (MN4 rank 1: one process; the GPU-holding `Worker pid=3000772` never recorded). Its host's `process_residency` named none of them (vLLM) and its saver enrollment was refused `owner_not_a_launch_member` (SGLang; `failed_rank` null, silent). The worker's Launch now waits, bounded by its deadline, until its tree holds one process per local rank besides the spawned one (none for a TensorFold follower), then settles as before (`capyctl-adapters` `group::worker_spawned`). The same record is what cleanup and supervision act on. Found live at MN2 with that wait: SGLang's worker forks a short-lived child before its rank processes, and each scan named children `worker-<i>` by start order among those alive, so the rank process was journaled as a second `worker-0` beside the gone child and the controller refused the member's identities (`mark_member_launched`: Plan). A journaled process now keeps its recorded role on every scan and a new one takes the next unused index of its kind (`journal::stable_roles`, persisted in the launch journal, so a restarted host role names them the same); the gone child stays recorded, under its own role, as gone.
- A vLLM member was judged on its processes' device memory plus anonymous pages against its 2 GiB parked placeholder, which a parked vLLM never meets on a unified host (MN4 vLLM: `failed_rank` 0, `group_member_failed`). Park and wake are now judged on the device memory the driver attributes to the member's processes (what the collective releases); SGLang stays on its saver map.
- The coordinator waited for member evidence until the step's deadline. It now waits a bounded window after the head's reply (60 s): a member still resident fails the group (`group_member_failed`), a member with no evidence of its own is `group_member_uncertain`; charges are retained either way and the group stop settles each member on its own host's evidence.

Each member's park also needs the residue its own processes hold in a host sample taken after the head's reply; for a revision whose phases CapyCTL derived, it is recorded for the revision on that member's host and for the launch (ADR 0014 amendments A13, A19) and the member is charged it instead of the placeholder (never above its Ready charge). A19's growth bound applies per member against its own host's `parked_growth_limit`. Declared `resources` keep their declared parked phase.

Tests (T20, T22, T31, T32): `a_worker_reports_its_rank_process_forked_after_its_imports` and `a_worker_without_its_ranks_by_the_deadline_is_uncertain` (adapters); `a_child_after_a_gone_transient_one_never_takes_its_role` (agent journal) and `a_gone_transient_child_never_lends_its_role_to_the_rank_process` (agent, a fake worker forking a child that exits before its rank process; failed before with two `worker-1/worker-0` roles, as live); `a_park_settles_on_released_device_memory_and_charges_each_members_residue` and `a_member_without_evidence_of_its_own_fails_the_park_within_the_window` in the group world, for vLLM and SGLang. CPU and Fake-engine tests are not qualification. Live check outstanding: MN2 (the SGLang group reaches Ready, host B's journal names every process under a distinct role), and MN4 vLLM and SGLang; the park reaches `parked` within about a minute of the head's call, each member charged its measured residue (status `held.member_owners` footprints above 2 GiB), the wake and canary pass, and no `owner_not_a_launch_member` appears in host B's log.

# Release note: Multi-node groups

- **A deep group parks.** A vLLM or SGLang group's park completes once each host proves its own rank released its memory, and each member is then charged what it really holds parked. A worker's recorded processes now include the rank process that holds the GPU, and a member that cannot prove its state fails the park within a minute instead of fifteen, with every charge kept until the group stop releases it.
